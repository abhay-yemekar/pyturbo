# Community expectations

Treat contributors and users with respect. Explain technical disagreements with
evidence, accept constructive feedback, and focus criticism on the work. People
of different backgrounds and experience levels are welcome.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized behavior,
deliberate disruption, and sharing another person's private information are not
acceptable.

The maintainer may edit or remove harmful contributions, close disruptive
discussions, or restrict participation to protect the community. Apply these
expectations consistently and consider the context and impact of reports.

Report concerns through an available private channel to
[abhay-yemekar](https://github.com/abhay-yemekar). If no private channel is available,
request one without posting sensitive details publicly. Security problems follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
